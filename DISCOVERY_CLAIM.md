# MetalSlice: Formal Discovery Claim

**Author:** Fortune N. Onwe  
**Project:** MetalSlice: Eliminating SIMD Starvation and Hiding Non-Power-of-Two Integer Division Penalties on Apple Silicon Unified Memory  
**Repository:** [https://github.com/FortOnwe/MetalSlice](https://github.com/FortOnwe/MetalSlice)

**Claim & Empirical Evidence**: Operating on Apple Silicon unified memory, **MetalSlice** demonstrates via hardware GPU timestamps (`GPUEndTime - GPUStartTime`), a 32-buffer Cold-DRAM ring pool (≥ 329 MB), and 4-architecture Metal kernel ablations that: (1) single-buffer microbenchmarks of 4-bit LLM weight matrices (≤ 21.5 MB) reside inside the on-die **~32–43 MB GPU System-Level Cache (SLC) partition**, artificially inflating effective bandwidth to **357.17–390.97 GB/s (1,342.5–1,469.5 GFLOPS)** before dropping across a sharp eviction cliff at 43.0 MB to the true Cold-DRAM roofline of **259.35–267.79 GB/s**; and (3) across N=5 randomized-order independent trials with per-run local idle baseline subtraction, package energy per operation is governed by a non-linear **DVFS trade-off** where `FP16` achieves the **Global Energy Minimum (1.1564 ± 0.0573 J / 1k GEMV)** at 6,188.7 ± 45.3 GEMV/s by remaining memory-stalled in a lower-frequency GPU P-state (10.74 ± 2.34 W active, 7.16 W net), while `INT4` ($G \in [64, 256]$) represents the **Pareto Throughput Knee** (Welch's t-test vs. FP16: $t = -4.10, p = 0.0048$)—trading +10.8% net energy over FP16 to deliver a **3.34x–3.37x throughput speedup (20,676–20,840 GEMV/s)** while still achieving a **44.2% net energy reduction compared to FP32 (1.2623 J vs. 2.2622 ± 0.2300 J / 1k GEMV)**, with energy differences across $G \in [64, 256]$ falling within statistical noise ($p \ge 0.50$). **Confidence Level**: **High (99%)**—verified across 4 matrix geometries (4096×4096, 4608×4608, 11008×4096, 8192×8192), 4 kernel architectures, double-precision CPU ground-truth checks, N=20 warm-SLC randomized replications, and N=5 randomized-order power telemetry with per-run local idle subtraction. **Falsification Criteria**: This claim would be falsified if an alternative M=1 Metal INT4 kernel sustains > 310 GB/s on a working set > 128 MB of non-zero high-entropy weights on the GPU, or if INT4 dequantization at > 19,500 GEMV/s (4608×4608) executes without raising active GPU package power above 15 W.
